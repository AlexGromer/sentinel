# GAPS — Sentinel

> 🌐 [Русский](GAPS.md) (основная версия) · **English**

Tracking of open questions, items to verify, and known risks. Format: `GAP-[CAT]-[NUM]`.
Categories: ARCH (architecture), VERIFY (needs fact-check), RISK, AGENT (missing tooling), DECISION (awaiting user).

> **One way to write an identifier (adopted 2026-08-21, `[DOCS-REGISTERS]`).** In the register and in any reference
> from a document or from code, the identifier is written IN FULL — `GAP-RISK-003`, never `RISK-003`. The short form
> was a historical abbreviation and it desynchronised the registers: a grep for `GAP-RISK-003` missed half the
> mentions. The code already used the full form (`brain/replay.py`, `pw-executor/src/determinism.ts`,
> `cmd/control-api/main.go`), so the canonical spelling was chosen from the code, not by a vote of documents.
> The square-bracket tags in `BACKLOG.md` (`[RISK-002]`, `[ARCH-001]`, `[PROTO-STUB-GATE]`) are names of UNITS OF
> WORK — a DIFFERENT namespace: a unit of work executes a register item and names it explicitly, but it is not the
> register identifier.

> Source: design workflow synthesis (2026-06-23) + BUILD-ONLY constraint reconciliation.
